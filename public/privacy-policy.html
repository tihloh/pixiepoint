Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

PixiePoint provides hotspot access, PisoWiFi/vendo integration, voucher and session management, device linking, points, and account features. This Privacy Policy explains what information PixiePoint accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares, including information received when you choose to sign in with Google.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use PixiePoint, we may process account information such as your name and email address; hotspot and device information such as MAC address, IP address, logical device UUID, router or interface context, and user agent; voucher and session information; points and account activity; and technical or security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Google user data

If you choose Continue with Google, PixiePoint requests only the OpenID Connect scopes openid, email, and profile. From Google, PixiePoint may receive your Google account identifier, verified email address, display name, and profile picture.

PixiePoint uses this Google user data only to authenticate you, create or link your PixiePoint account, display basic account information, associate your devices and account benefits with you, and protect the service from account misuse. PixiePoint does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts, or other Google product data through this sign-in flow.

The Google OAuth access token is used to retrieve your basic profile during sign-in and is not intentionally stored by PixiePoint after the sign-in flow. PixiePoint stores only the account information needed to maintain your PixiePoint account, such as your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile picture URL.

How we use information

We use information to provide hotspot access and account features; authorize vouchers and sessions; remember linked devices; maintain points and rewards; support users and operators; detect abuse, fraud, and security problems; troubleshoot the service; and meet legal obligations.

How we share information

PixiePoint does not sell Google user data or personal information. Information may be shared only as needed with service providers that help operate PixiePoint, with the operator or organization responsible for the hotspot you are using where necessary to provide the service, when you direct us to share it, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect users, PixiePoint, or others.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PixiePoint's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

PixiePoint stores account and service data on systems used to operate the service. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide your account and hotspot services, maintain security and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Data that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and controls

You may use supported guest hotspot features without registering where the hotspot operator allows it. You may revoke PixiePoint's Google access from your Google Account permissions. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your PixiePoint account information by contacting the PixiePoint operator or support contact provided through the service. Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Third-party services

PixiePoint may depend on third-party services such as Google authentication, network equipment, hosting providers, and payment or vendo services. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies when you interact with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PixiePoint's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published. We will keep this page consistent with how Google user data and other personal information are actually handled by the service.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the PixiePoint operator or the support contact provided within the PixiePoint service.
